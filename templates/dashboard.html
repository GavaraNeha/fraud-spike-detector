Why a Tabular ML Model over an LLM for Payment Risk?

Payment checkout scoring is latency-sensitive (<50ms target) and requires 100% deterministic, audit-traceable outputs for compliance reviewers. A gradient boosting model evaluates numeric velocity signals in sub-milliseconds with exact feature importance weights — providing reliability and speed an LLM call cannot match.
